# Privacy Policy, Fourth Dimension

Last updated: 13 October 2026

1. **Who we are.** Fourth Dimension is a private tool of an online store in Iraq. Contact: thefourth4d@gmail.com.
2. **What we collect.** When the store owner connects their TikTok account, the App receives TikTok's basic profile information (account identifier and display name) and an access token. It collects nothing from other people or from viewers of the store's videos.
3. **Why.** Only to publish the store owner's own videos and captions to the store owner's own TikTok account.
4. **Storage.** The access token is stored on a private computer owned by the store owner. It is not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.
5. **Retention and deletion.** The token is kept only while the account is connected. The store owner can disconnect at any time in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions, Manage app permissions), and can ask for deletion of all stored data by writing to thefourth4d@gmail.com; it will be deleted.
6. **Children.** The App is not directed at children and collects no data about them.
7. **Changes.** This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
